Physics & SDE Formulation ​

This page introduces Parker's transport equation (PTE), explains the physical meaning and regime of validity of each term, and derives the equivalent forward-in-time Itô stochastic differential equation (SDE) solved by ParticleTransport.jl.

Physical Regime & Assumptions ​

Charged energetic particles (such as solar energetic particles, anomalous cosmic rays, and galactic cosmic rays) propagate through magnetized plasmas whose bulk flow and magnetic field vary on macroscopic length scales . In this regime, resolving individual gyro-orbits with a Lorentz solver like TestParticle.jl becomes prohibitively expensive over macroscopic transport times  . Instead, ParticleTransport.jl advances the macroscopic distribution directly via equivalent stochastic trajectories.
